# Joye Life v3.0.10

## Paycheck planner correction

- Removed the automatically generated **Money left to plan** allocation.
- Unassigned paycheck funds now remain in the **Still available** total.
- The planner only imports actual recurring bills and debt minimum payments due before the next paycheck.
- Users can manually assign remaining funds to savings, groceries, extra debt, spending, or another category.
- Improved the empty-state and remaining-balance explanations.
